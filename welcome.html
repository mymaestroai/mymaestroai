Welcome

To keep anyone else on this computer from claiming your hub, the first account needs the setup link the installer printed. If you closed that window, open a terminal on the computer running My Maestro AI and type:

mymaestro setup-link

Create your account

Connect your first AI

Your agents need at least one AI model to think with. Pick one way now; you can add the others later in Settings.

  1. Install Claude Code on this computer:
    curl -fsSL https://claude.ai/install.sh | bash
  2. Open a new terminal window, type claude and press Enter. Choose your Claude account, sign in in the browser, then type /exit.
  3. Press Check. My Maestro AI asks Claude a one-word question to confirm it works.

Claude's sign-in opens in your browser and may take over this page. That's fine: once Claude says you're signed in, press Back or open this hub's address again. This page continues at this step and checks Claude by itself.

Meet Maestro

Maestro is your lead agent, and it knows how My Maestro AI works. Next it interviews you for a few minutes: who you are, which agents you'd like (a Doc, a lawyer, a developer, a personal assistant…), their models and personalities. Then it builds them, shows you what each can do, and helps you set up the phone app.

You can change this any time from the chat header.

The interview continues in the app. The ? button at the top right has how-tos and help if anything goes wrong.
